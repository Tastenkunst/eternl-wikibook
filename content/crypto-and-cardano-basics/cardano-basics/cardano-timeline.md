# Cardano Timeline

Cardano launched in 2017 and has evolved through a series of network upgrades. These changes introduced features such as staking, native tokens, smart contracts and on-chain governance. Development continues, so the milestones below are an introduction rather than a complete history or a schedule of future releases.

## Development Phases

Cardano's roadmap describes five broad development themes. They are useful for understanding the goals behind the network's evolution, but work on them can overlap and continues beyond any single upgrade.

- **Byron:** The launch and initial operation of the Cardano network.
- **Shelley:** Decentralization of block production and the introduction of staking and delegation.
- **Goguen:** Native assets and smart contract capabilities for building decentralized applications.
- **Basho:** Scalability, performance and interoperability.
- **Voltaire:** Community-led governance, including on-chain decisions about protocol changes and treasury spending.

These themes reflect a long-term vision: a network that can support useful applications, grow to serve more people and evolve through community participation. They should not be confused with ledger eras or individual upgrades, which have their own names. A theme is not necessarily finished simply because another has begun.

## Wallets and Explorers

The early Cardano ecosystem included both [Daedalus](https://daedaluswallet.io/) and Yoroi. Daedalus is a **full-node wallet**: it downloads the blockchain and validates it locally, requiring substantial storage and synchronization time. A **light wallet**, such as Eternl or Yoroi, does not need to download and validate the full chain itself; it obtains blockchain information through other services.

Blockchain explorers provide another view of public information, including transactions, addresses and blocks. You can find examples in [Cardano Ecosystem Resources](/crypto-and-cardano-basics/cardano-ecosystem-resources.md).

## Selected Milestones

- **2017 – Cardano launches:** The Byron era establishes the network and ADA as its native currency.
- **2020 – Shelley:** Delegation and stake pools become part of the network, enabling a transition toward decentralized block production.
- **2021 – Mary and Alonzo:** Mary introduces native tokens; Alonzo adds Plutus smart contract functionality.
- **2022 – Vasil:** An upgrade introduces improvements for smart contracts and network performance.
- **2024–2025 – Chang and Plomin:** These upgrades introduce Cardano's on-chain governance system. Plomin activates the full set of governance actions and the DRep role, enabling the community to make decisions about protocol changes and treasury spending through the governance process. This marks a major milestone for Voltaire.

For an explanation of how these governance roles work, see [Governance & Catalyst](/crypto-and-cardano-basics/cardano-basics/governance-and-catalyst.md).
