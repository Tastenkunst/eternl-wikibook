# Cardano Ecosystem Resources

Explore these resources to learn about Cardano and find tools, projects, and community discussions. This is a selection of links, not a complete list. It is updated occasionally, so some information may be out of date.

## Blockchain Explorers

Blockchain explorers let you look up transactions, addresses, tokens, stake pools, and other on-chain information.

* [AdaStat](https://adastat.net/)
* [Cardanoscan](https://cardanoscan.io/)
* [Cexplorer](https://cexplorer.io/)
* [Pool.pm](https://pool.pm/)

## Staking

Explore stake pools and staking statistics. The blockchain explorers listed above also provide information about stake pools.

* [PoolTool](https://pooltool.io/)

## Governance and Community Funding

Explore DReps, governance actions, community discussions, and funding proposals.

* [DRepTalk](https://dreptalk.com/)
* [GovTool](https://gov.tools/)
* [Project Catalyst](https://projectcatalyst.io/)
