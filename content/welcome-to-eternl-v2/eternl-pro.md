# Eternl Pro [[ProButton]]

**Eternl Pro** offers advanced tools for power users, DReps, traders, teams, and developers, while supporting Eternl's ongoing operation and long-term, independent development.

:::info
Core wallet functionality, such as sending and receiving assets, staking, basic dApp connections, and standard account management, remains **100% free** for all users.
:::

---

## Why Eternl Pro? (Path to Sustainability)

Eternl has grown into a core gateway for Cardano, including everyday asset transfers, DeFi, NFTs, governance, and dApps. Maintaining high-performance node infrastructure, fast sync speeds, continuous wallet updates, and dedicated user support requires significant ongoing operational resources and infrastructure costs.

Historically, community tools heavily relied on Treasury grants (Catalyst/Open Source funding). However, relying solely on grant allocations can be competitive, political, and uncertain.

To ensure **financial independence, long-term stability, and continuous innovation** without relying on external funding or resorting to ads, Eternl established a self-sustaining model:

* **Operations & Sustainability:** Pro license revenue supports server infrastructure, RPC node operations, ongoing maintenance, and future feature development.
* **Feature-Driven Monetization:** Essential wallet features remain free, while Pro plans provide specialized power-user tools, team workflows, and advanced developer services.
* **Privacy & Independence:** Eternl remains non-custodial and ad-free. Your data belongs to you.

---

## Pro Plans & Scope

Eternl Pro licenses are applied to the currently selected **Entity** and support both individual and organization use cases:

* **Individual Yearly ($96 USD / year):** Designed for individual power users. Allows usage on up to **three Eternl app instances**.
* **Business Yearly ($360 USD / year):** Designed for companies, teams, and institutions. Includes a minimum of **3 seats** with easy address-based seat management and option to add more seats on demand.

---

## Eternl Pro Feature Set

Eternl Pro unlocks an extensive suite of advanced tools across the application:

* **Eternl Analytics:** Explore your portfolio, Cardano market data, tokens, and wallet activity with Portfolio, Discover, Token Analytics, Wallet Profiler, and Multichart.
* **Eternl Hub:** Pair Eternl instances to share wallets and settings and request remote signatures. The instance creating the pairing code needs Pro; the joining instance does not.
* **Multi-sig Wallets:** Creation of both simple and advanced Multi-sig wallets, as well as importing existing Multi-sig wallets.
* **Governance Tools:** Voting, proposal creation, DRep registration, and metadata pinning for active governance participants.
* **Developer & Power-User Tools:**
    * Transaction viewer details & UTxO inspection per address
    * Franken address creation & Address inspection
    * Token inspector & Credential conversions
    * Manual `signData` signing and verification
    * Calidus key assistance & CLI key import
    * Read-only account key and address wallet imports
* **Cross-Chain Bridge:** Direct USDCx to Ethereum bridging functionality.
* **Convenience Utilities:** Global search across the wallet and custom Transaction notes.
* **Themes:** Access to additional themes, including Classic and Monochrome.

:::tip
**Testing Pro Features:** You don't always need an active Pro subscription to test upcoming tools. Pro features are frequently unlocked for free for a limited time inside the **Eternl Beta extension** for community testing!
:::

## Privacy

Eternl remains a self-custody wallet. Your recovery phrase and private keys are not shared when you purchase or activate Eternl Pro.

Eternl Pro uses wallet signatures to verify control of the License Account. After purchase, a license activation transaction is recorded on-chain. Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments, which processes the information required for payment.

---

## How to Activate Eternl Pro

You can purchase an Eternl Pro license directly within Eternl by opening the **Pro plan** window.

Payment processing and checkout are handled by **Dodo Payments** as the Merchant of Record. Available payment methods, including stablecoins where offered, are shown in checkout. If you already hold an active license on another device, click **Check Pro Access** inside the modal to activate Pro in this instance.

For information about checking access, purchase, and device activation, see [Activating Eternl Pro](/using-eternl/activating-eternl-pro.md).
