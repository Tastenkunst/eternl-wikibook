# How Do I Find My Receiving Address?

## On Desktop

In the desktop application, click the **Receive** tab in the main menu on the left. Your current receiving address and the corresponding QR code will be displayed.

## On Mobile

On the main screen of the mobile app, tap **Receive** in the card showing your account. Your receiving address and the corresponding QR code will be displayed.
