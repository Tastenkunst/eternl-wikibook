# Activating Eternl Pro [[ProButton]](/welcome-to-eternl-v2/eternl-pro.md)

This guide explains how to purchase an Eternl Pro license and activate an existing license in another Eternl instance. For an overview of Eternl Pro, its purpose, and its features, see [Eternl Pro](/welcome-to-eternl-v2/eternl-pro.md).

:::info
The license is linked to a License Account and applies to the currently selected entity. Pro features are available for all wallets and accounts in that entity.
:::

## Buy and Activate a Pro License {open}

1. Click the **Pro** button in the Top Bar to open the **Pro plan** window.
2. **Select a Plan** (currently **Individual Yearly** or **Business Yearly**).
3. Select the account you want to use as the **License Account**.
4. Click **Check Pro Access**.
5. **Sign** the **wallet proof**.
6. If no existing license is found, the **Dodo Payments checkout** opens in a new tab. Review the purchase details and complete the payment.
7. Return to Eternl. Once payment is confirmed, follow the prompts to **sign and submit** the on-chain license activation transaction.
8. Wait for the transaction to be confirmed, then **sign** another **wallet proof** to activate Pro in this Eternl instance.

You can also select a plan after signing the wallet proof. In that case, choose a plan after Eternl checks for existing Pro access, then continue to checkout.

:::info
The wallet proof confirms that you control the keys for the selected License Account. Signing it is **not** an on-chain transaction and does not make a payment or move any funds.
:::

:::info
The later on-chain license activation requires a network fee. Make sure the License Account has enough ADA to cover it.
:::

Read-only and multi-sig accounts cannot be used as License Accounts.

Dodo Payments determines the available payment methods, which may include card payments, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Amazon Pay, WeChat, and stablecoins. The options shown may vary by location and can change over time.

If you paid but did not complete the on-chain activation, reopen the **Pro plan** window and follow the prompts to continue. If you need to find the purchase again, select the same License Account and use **Check Pro Access** or **Find paid purchase** if shown. Sign the wallet proof and follow the prompts to resume the activation. If the purchase cannot be found, contact support before starting another checkout.

## Activate an Existing Pro License

If your account has an active Pro license or a Business seat, you can activate Pro in another Eternl instance. An Individual Pro license can be activated on up to three Eternl instances, for example on your desktop and mobile devices.

1. Make sure the account linked to the license or Business seat is available in this Eternl instance.
2. Click the **Pro** button in the Top Bar to open the **Pro plan** window, then select that account as the **License Account**.
3. Click **Check Pro Access** and sign the wallet proof.
4. Follow the prompts to activate Pro in this instance, including signing the wallet proof when requested.

Once Pro is activated, the **Pro icon** appears next to the entity name in the Top Bar.
