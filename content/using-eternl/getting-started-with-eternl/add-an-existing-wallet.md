# Add an Existing Wallet

You can add an existing wallet to Eternl using its seed phrase or connect a hardware wallet.

If this is your first time using Eternl, complete the [initial setup](/using-eternl/getting-started-with-eternl/set-up-eternl-and-create-a-wallet.md#set-up-eternl) first. After setup, Eternl opens the wallet selection screen.

## Restore a Wallet With a Seed Phrase

:::warning
Only enter your seed phrase in an Eternl app or website you trust. Never share it with anyone. If you use a hardware wallet, connect the device instead of entering its seed phrase into Eternl.
:::

1. If the wallet selection screen is not already open, click **Add wallet** at the bottom of the wallet list.
2. Select **Enter a Seed-phrase**.
3. Choose the number of words in your seed phrase.
4. Enter the words in the correct order and click **Next**.
5. Automatic account discovery is enabled by default. To specify the number of accounts instead, turn it off and choose a number.
6. Enter a name for the wallet.
7. Set a strong spending password and follow the instructions to complete the restoration.

The spending password you set here is used in this Eternl instance. It does not have to be the password used in another wallet app.

If an account is not shown after restoring your wallet, you can [add it later](/using-eternl/manage-accounts.md#add-an-account) using its account index.

## Connect a Hardware Wallet

1. If the wallet selection screen is not already open, click **Add wallet** at the bottom of the wallet list.
2. Select **Connect Hardware**.
3. Choose your hardware wallet type.
4. Automatic account discovery is enabled by default. To specify the number of accounts instead, turn it off and choose a number.
5. Follow the instructions in Eternl and on your hardware wallet to connect it and approve the requested action.
6. Enter or review the wallet name, then complete the setup.

:::info
Depending on your hardware wallet and its firmware, there may be limits on the number of accounts and the account indexes you can use.
:::

The exact connection steps depend on the device. Your hardware wallet is needed to authorize transactions from the connected wallet.
